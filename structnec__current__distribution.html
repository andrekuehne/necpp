Detailed Description

Owned exact physical A/B/C current coefficients and segment geometry.

Geometry is in metres and coefficients are in amperes. NEC's native wavelength-normalized coefficient storage is converted at capture time. Coefficient planes are mode-major: index = modeIndex * nSegments + segmentIndex.
