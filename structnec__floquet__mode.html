Detailed Description

One Floquet mode radiated by the unit cell, with one amplitude per port.

Above the cell the field is the sum of the modes E exp(-j (kt . r + kz z)), below it exp(-j (kt . r - kz z)), with r the absolute position, so the phase is referred to the origin as in NEC's far fields. te and tm are the components along phi-hat and theta-hat of the mode direction (kt, +-kz) (phi = 0 when kt = 0), power-normalized: a propagating mode carries |te|^2 / 2 + |tm|^2 / 2 watts per cell, an evanescent mode as much reactive power (|kz| in place of kz).
