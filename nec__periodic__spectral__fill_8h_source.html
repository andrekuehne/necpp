2// The spectral Ewald sum's share of the periodic fill, evaluated per Floquet
3// mode instead of per kernel sample.
4//
5// The fill needs the segment field (segment_field_of_kernel) of every source
6// segment under periodic_green::spectral at every observation point, and over
7// perfect ground that of the segment's ground image. One mode's term at
8// r = observation - source point is
9//
10// exp(-j kt . rho_obs) exp(+j kt . rho_src) F(dz),
11//
12// with its gradient from F and a second factor G (spectral_factor): the error
13// functions of the mode sit in F and G, which depend on the height
14// difference dz alone. So the factors of a mode are evaluated once per scan
15// state, on a table in dz, and every pair reads them from it; the transverse
16// phases factor into an observation and a source part. A cell whose dz takes
17// few distinct values (a planar cell: 0 and the image heights) evaluates them
18// at those values exactly. A cell whose table would exceed max_table_entries
19// (nodes times modes) evaluates every kernel sample directly, as
20// periodic_green::spectral does.
21//
22// The table interpolates with centred 8-point Lagrange stencils
23// (nec_periodic_interpolation.h). Per scan state it compares the midpoints of
24// the intervals in use with direct values and reports the largest error of
25// the kernel (value and gradient), relative to the size 1 / (2 A |k|) of one
26// propagating mode's term: an estimate on the scale of the kernel's own
27// accuracy (kernel_error).
