Detailed Description

Periodic mode: a layered stack above the wires (a radome), in metres, layers from its lower face up, with air above it. Each Floquet mode sees it through a TE and a TM reflection added to the interaction matrix, and the modes above it are the waves it transmits. Applied from the next stateful_prepare_frequency(); the scan-independent fill is kept.
