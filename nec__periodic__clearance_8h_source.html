2// Clearance between the conductors of a unit cell and their periodic images,
3// and the images close enough to need NEC's thin-wire segment field.
4//
5// The periodic solver models arrays whose cells are electrically separate. A
6// conductor that touches, or nearly touches, a periodic image (its own or
7// that of another conductor in the cell) would form a connected array, which
8// the solver does not support. The check measures distances to images, not
9// to a cell wall, which has no unique shape for a non-rectangular lattice.
